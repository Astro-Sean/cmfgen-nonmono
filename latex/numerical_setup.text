Numerical Setup

We model the double-explosion scenario with a two-stage pipeline coupling the
multi-group radiation hydrodynamics code STELLA
 with the non-LTE radiative transfer code CMFGEN
Hillier1998, Dessart2015.  In the first stage (SNA), a Type IIb
progenitor - the solar-metallicity  model s12.0 of
WoosleyHeger2007, stripped to a 3 helium core and
matched to model 12C of for SN2011dh - is exploded with a
thermal bomb of erg deposited over 10s in the inner
0.3, yielding a final kinetic energy of 0.7foe.  A thin
hydrogen envelope of 0.03-0.10 () is added to the surface,
and a low-density () sigmoid-blended
circumstellar wind extends to cm for numerical stability.  A
 mass of 0.075 is distributed over the inner
0.3.  STELLA evolves the explosion with 500 Lagrangian zones using a
GEAR integrator (), with  to preserve the
pre-SN velocity structure,  for frequency-dependent
opacity, and a minimum of  diffusion zones to prevent
late-time  collapse.

In the second stage (SNB), the SNA ejecta is homologously evolved to
400d and serves as the CSM.  A H/He shell (1.0, ,
) with a power-law density profile () following
Dessart2016 is placed at the centre and launched at 5000 into
the CSM.  STELLA evolves the shell-CSM interaction for 45d with
 (preserving both shell and CSM velocities), enhanced artificial
viscosity () for the strong velocity reversal, and no  (the
luminosity is entirely interaction-powered).

At selected epochs (30d post-SBO for SNA; 20-40d post-launch for SNB),
snapshots are extracted from the STELLA output and remapped onto CMFGEN depth
grids () using an adaptive algorithm that resolves ionisation
fronts and velocity reversals.  CMFGEN computes non-LTE level populations and
emergent spectra with VELLAW=12 (reading the non-homologous
velocity directly), a tridiagonal depth-coupling solver for the non-monotonic
velocity, Ng acceleration disabled to avoid trace-species instability at
ionisation fronts, and temperature fixed to the hydrodynamic values.  The
inner boundary uses a diffusion condition seeded by the STELLA luminosity.
The complete pipeline - progenitor conversion, STELLA evolution, snapshot
extraction, CMFGEN spectral synthesis, and dependency-chained job submission
- is orchestrated by a single workflow manager on the MPCDF Viper HPC
cluster.
